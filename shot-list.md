Seven beats, 10 seconds, matching the original storyboard timings.
Two shots are already rendered; five remain.

**Continuity rule:** append the STYLE BLOCK to every prompt, verbatim, on every
shot. Independent generations drift — identical style language is what stops the

## STYLE BLOCK — append to all seven prompts

> Look: cinematic premium product commercial, warm neutral colour grade, subtle
> film grain, rich contrast, shallow depth of field with creamy bokeh falloff.
> Consistent set throughout: a matte black home espresso machine with polished
> chrome group head and a slim stainless steel steam wand, standing on a warm
> walnut wood counter, against an exposed red brick wall, with a tall window of
> sheer cream linen curtains off to the right casting soft diffused
> late-afternoon daylight from camera right and deep soft shadow to camera left.
> The cup throughout is one small dark green glazed ceramic cup.
> Strictly no text of any kind, no logos, no brand names, no model names, no
> lettering, no screen or interface graphics, no captions or overlays, no
> people, no hands, no human reflections.

## Platform notes

**Durations.** Shots 2, 3, 6 and 7 are one-second beats in the storyboard, but no
model will render one second usefully. Generate each at the model minimum (4s on
minimax-h3 / veo-3.1 / sora-2; 3s on gemini-omni-flash; 1s on seedance-2) and
trim on the timeline. Generate long, cut short.

**Model minimums.** vidIQ charges a 60-credit floor per generation regardless of
model or duration — so five remaining shots costs 300 credits there, against a
150/month allowance. Higgsfield bills separately and is the cheaper route to a
complete set.

**Seeding.** Slice the seven panels out of the storyboard contact sheet and feed
each shot its own panel as a start frame. Same cost, far tighter match to the
composition already drawn, and it solves continuity better than prompt text can.

**Editing.** Hard cut on every beat — no dissolves. The sequence is fast and the
cuts should feel deliberate. Shots 1 and 7 share framing, so cutting from 6 to 7
lands as a return rather than a new setup.
