How ordering works

Four taps, no queue.

  1. Add what you like

    Tap Add on any drink or bake. Milk drinks let you choose whole, oat or almond first.

  2. Check your order

    Open Your order to change quantities or take anything out.

  3. Pick a bar and a time

    Choose where to collect it, and whether you want it now or in 15 or 30 minutes.

  4. Collect at the bar

    We start the shot so it is fresh when you arrive, and call your name at the bar.
